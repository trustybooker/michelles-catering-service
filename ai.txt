# Michelle's Catering Service

Michelle's Catering Service provides Jamaican catering and global comfort food in Port St. Lucie, Stuart, Vero Beach, and the Treasure Coast of Florida.

Services: weddings, birthdays, family reunions, graduations, corporate events, office catering, private dinners, and celebration buffets.

Popular menu direction: jerk chicken, oxtail, curry goat, brown stew, rice and peas, and other Jamaican-inspired dishes.
